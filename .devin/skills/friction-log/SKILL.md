# Log friction

Append one entry to `docs/friction-log.md` when a task hit avoidable friction: a missing or wrong instruction, a flaky check, a slow tool, an unclear rule, a repeated manual step.

```
## YYYY-MM-DD — <short title>
- What happened: <1-2 lines, facts>
- Cost: <rounds/time lost>
- Suggested fix: <skill/script/rule change, concrete>
```

Keep entries short and factual. Review the log periodically and turn repeated items into skill/script changes.
